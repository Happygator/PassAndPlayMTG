Support

Sealed Pass-and-Play is a two-player Magic: The Gathering sealed game for a single shared device. One phone, two players, passed back and forth.

Getting started

  1. Pick a mode: 3-Card Blind or Pai Gow MTG.
  2. Pick a cube and set the pool size and number of decks per player.
  3. Hand the device to player one, who holds the reveal button to see their sealed pool and builds their decks.
  4. Pass the device to player two and repeat.
  5. Play the matches and record each result. The app tallies the outcome.

Common questions

Does it need an internet connection? It depends on the mode. Cube games need none at all — those cards and their images ship inside the app. Booster games and 3-Card Blind draw on far more cards than could ever ship inside an app, so their card images are fetched from Scryfall as they are shown, and kept on your device afterwards. 3-Card Blind's card search also queries Scryfall as you type.

Where did my game go? An unfinished game is saved on your device and offered again next time you open the app, as a "Game in progress" banner on the start screen. It stays until you resume it, discard it, or finish it. Results are not kept once a game is over.

A card image is missing or wrong. Card data comes from Scryfall. Please report it using the link below and name the card.

Can I use my own cube? The web version ships with fixed cubes. Adding your own cube list is supported in the app version.

Report a problem or request a feature

Please open an issue at github.com/Happygator/PassAndPlayMTG/issues.

Include your device and operating system version, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots help.
