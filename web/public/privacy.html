Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Sealed Pass-and-Play does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device so that it can work offline and remember what you were doing:

  • The bundled cubes and their card images, so cube games work without a connection.
  • Card images downloaded while you play, kept so they are not fetched twice.
  • Your in-progress game and your last-used game settings.
  • Any custom banlists or cube lists you create or import.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, removes all of it permanently.

Network connections

Whether a connection is needed depends on which mode you play. Every request the app makes is for publicly available Magic: The Gathering card information; none of them carry any information about you.

  • Cube games need no connection at all. The cube's cards and their images ship inside the app.
  • Booster games and 3-Card Blind draw on far more cards than could ever ship inside an app, so their card images are requested from Scryfall as they are shown and then kept on your device. 3-Card Blind's card search also queries Scryfall while you type.
  • Loading or updating the app, including refreshing the banlist and the list of sets it ships with.
  • Importing a cube, if you choose to do so. Importing sends the deck list address you provide to that deck-hosting service, and requests card data and card images from Scryfall.

There is no account, no identifier and no server of ours involved in any of this, so none of it tells us anything — including whether the app was ever opened.

These third-party services receive the ordinary technical information any web request includes, such as your device's IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies. We receive nothing.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
